Board Game Maroe

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: Board Game Maroe is a free hobby project with no ads and no analytics. You can play without an account. Data is stored on our servers only if you sign in with Google, and then only to the extent described below. We do not sell your data or share it for any other purpose.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your nickname, avatar, statistics, daily challenge results, and settings (such as display and sound preferences) in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device and is lost if you clear this site's data in your browser.

2. Signing in with Google (optional)

Signing in with Google is optional and lets your statistics follow you to other devices. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service.

  • What Firebase receives from your Google account: your email address, name, and profile photo. We never receive your Google password.
  • What the game stores in its database (Firestore): your nickname, avatar, the date you started playing, your per-game statistics, and the time of your last update.
  • Your email address is not shown to other players and is not written to the game's database.

3. Leaderboards (public)

If you sign in with Google and record a score, your nickname, avatar, and best score can be seen by all players on the leaderboard. If you do not want your real name or other personal information to be visible, do not use it as your nickname.

4. Online play

  • To play online, the game automatically creates an anonymous account containing a random ID. It contains no personal data.
  • Game moves are sent through the database (Firestore) as short messages tagged with a room code. These messages contain only move data and expire after 6 hours.
  • Do not share your room code with people you do not know.

5. Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google): sign-in, database, and delivery of Firebase scripts. Google applies its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: used to load typefaces. As with most websites, this makes your IP address visible to Google.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this site. GitHub may keep standard access logs.

This site does not use advertising, ad trackers, or analytics services.

6. Deleting your data

  • Local data: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Account data: to have your account and data (nickname, avatar, statistics, and leaderboard scores) deleted from our servers, email maruhawa.stevanus@gmail.com and include your nickname.

7. Children

The game is open to a general audience, but we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Children should play without signing in, or with a parent's permission.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change if the game's features change. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email maruhawa.stevanus@gmail.com.
